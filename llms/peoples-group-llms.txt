# Peoples Group

> Peoples Group is the operating name of Peoples Trust Company, a Vancouver-based Canadian trust company and chartered-bank group (founded 1985, owned by the Triple Five Group) whose fintech and payments arm provides embedded banking-as-a-service to program managers, fintechs, brokers, and merchants across Canada. Its APIs are real and in production, but access is partnership- and sales-gated: there is no public self-serve developer portal, no downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger specification, and no public Postman workspace. Integration is arranged through a client relationship and the product documentation lives on the corporate fintech site.

## APIs

- [Interac e-Transfer API](https://www.peoplesgroup.com/fintech/solutions/payments/money-movement): API-driven Interac e-Transfer for Canadian money movement — Send Money, Request Money, Receive AutoDeposit, and Receive Question-and-Answer flows.
- [EFT API](https://www.peoplesgroup.com/fintech/solutions/payments/money-movement): Electronic Funds Transfer for pre-authorized debits and credits to and from Canadian-domiciled accounts in CAD and USD, with individual, batch, and recurring transactions.
- [Bill Pay API](https://www.peoplesgroup.com/fintech/solutions/payments/money-movement): Bill payment to more than 12,000 billers across Canada, with individual, batch, and recurring payments.
- [Push-to-Card API (Visa Direct & Mastercard Send)](https://www.peoplesgroup.com/fintech/solutions/payments/money-movement): Real-time push-to-card disbursements via Visa Direct and Mastercard Send for B2B and B2C payouts.
- [Card Issuing](https://www.peoplesgroup.com/fintech/solutions/payments/card-issuing): Prepaid and credit card issuing through Visa and Mastercard BIN sponsorship for fintech program managers.
